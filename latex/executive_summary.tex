rank between TS-ICL and Chronos-2.

\section{Univariate evidence}
Chronos-2 has lower aggregate nMSE than the best repeat, lookback, or mean
baseline on 153 of 189 tasks, with one tie. Its median relative improvement is
24.10\%. Across the nine Electricity, Solar, and Traffic publication settings,
the improvement ranges from 20.88\% to 51.26\%. Exchange Rate is the important
counterexample: persistence is better at all three settings, by 0.60\% to
4.88\%. Raw averages across all datasets are not reported because several TIME
tasks have near-zero normalization denominators and would dominate them.

\section{Controls and covariates}
Removing constant windows improves only 5 of 189 matched Chronos-2 tasks and
has zero median effect, although those five avoid extreme normalized-error
cases. Instance normalization improves 93 of 189 tasks and also has zero
median effect. Neither intervention is supported as a universal default.

The identity-covariate capability control beats the same model without
covariates on 185 of 189 Chronos-2 tasks and 176 of 189 TS-ICL tasks. Median
relative nMSE improvements are 97.93\% and 98.07\%, respectively. Identity
passes the target itself as a covariate, so this establishes adapter capability
rather than a deployable forecasting gain.

The synchronized covariate raw results are eligible, but the old report stage
grouped by covariate mode and then searched each group for a simple baseline.
Because that family contains only Chronos-2 and TS-ICL, the comparison tables
were empty. The corrected report pairs each augmented row with the same
backbone and task under \code{covariate_mode=none}; no inference rerun is
required.

\section{Foundation-model comparison}
Replacement job 2964473 completed 512 rows for 128 tasks, four models, and 64
datasets, superseding the failed evaluation and partial report. TS-ICL has the
lowest nMSE on 51 tasks, Chronos-2 on 50, Chronos-Bolt on 17, and Chronos-T5 on
10. Chronos-2 nevertheless has the best mean rank and beats TS-ICL
head-to-head on 69 of 128 tasks. The grid therefore identifies two strong
leaders under different summaries rather than one universal winner.

\section{Next evidence}
Deploy the report fix and rerun only the full covariate report stage. The
foundation evaluation and report are complete and require no rerun.
