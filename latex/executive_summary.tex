\section{Obtained evidence}
Historically, the single-seed full-univariate snapshot evaluated Electricity,
Solar, and Traffic at $168{:}24$, $336{:}48$, and $504{:}168$ with stride 512.
Chronos-2 beat the best non-trainable baseline in nMSE on all nine settings;
the mean improvements were 31.335\%, 43.099\%, and 37.394\%, respectively.
These identities consumed tensor inputs and therefore cannot be reused now.
The former Exchange Rate settings also used hourly scales on daily data.

At Electricity $504{:}168$, the historical four-model test did not establish a
metric-independent winner: Chronos-Bolt had the lowest nMSE, while TS-ICL had
the lowest raw MSE. The covariate family showed a large Chronos-2 identity
signal, but only three configurations completed; controls stopped after nine.
Both families are incomplete and pre-contract, so neither supports a current
conclusion. Two obsolete manifests remain marked running under Selena jobs
2910738 and 2910739, and DGX job 45673 was interrupted by missing Chronos-2
weights.

\section{Next evidence}
