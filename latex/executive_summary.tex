\section{Obtained evidence}
The completed full-univariate snapshot evaluated Electricity, Solar, and
Traffic at $168{:}24$, $336{:}48$, and $504{:}168$ with seed 1 and stride 512.
Chronos-2 beat the best persistence, expected, repeat, or weekly-lookback
baseline in nMSE on all nine configurations. Its mean nMSE improvement over the
best baseline was 31.335\% on Electricity, 43.099\% on Solar, and 37.394\% on
Traffic.

These are single-seed, sparsely sampled results. The warning about an unset
deterministic CUDA workspace variable further limits a claim of exact GPU
reproducibility. The former Exchange Rate results used hourly-scale
$L$--$H$ values on daily data and are excluded from the current protocol.

\section{Next evidence}
Resubmit full univariate under the cadence-aware profile. Exact completed hourly

regenerate the mixed-cadence report. Then complete full controls, covariates,
and foundation-model families before making claims about those questions.
